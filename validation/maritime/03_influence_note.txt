A little-known footnote to the Steadfast Compass Mount's history: decades later, a harbor engineer studying old catalog drawings adapted its self-leveling principle for an entirely different purpose. The result, the Harbor Beacon Leveler, kept a rotating lighthouse lens stable during storms by applying the same weighted-ring approach the compass mount used internally. The Harbor Beacon Leveler saw limited adoption, installed in only a handful of lighthouses before electric gyroscopic stabilizers made it obsolete.
