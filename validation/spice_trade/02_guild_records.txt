Charter and minutes of The Ashwick Kestrel Traders Guild (hereafter AKTG), transcribed from the original ledgers.

Founded by a small group of signatory traders under a shared warehouse and shipping arrangement, the AKTG grew within its first decade to include member houses across the region. The AKTG's collective bargaining power eventually let member traders negotiate fixed highland purchase prices, ending years of price volatility that had plagued traders working alone.

The guild's minutes record the appointment of Wren Renn, granddaughter of a founding highland contact unrelated to the guild's first master, as the guild's second master, following the death of the founding master. Under this new leadership, the AKTG expanded its warehouse capacity and opened a further trading post along the highland route. Guild records describe Renn as a steady hand in difficult seasons.

The AKTG continued operating as a formal cooperative until changing tariff law made the shared-warehouse arrangement impractical for its remaining member houses, at which point the cooperative was dissolved by unanimous vote.
